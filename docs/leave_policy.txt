LEAVE POLICY

ABC Limited is a fictional private organization providing IT services, software development, consulting, and business solutions.
This leave policy explains how employees can request, use, and manage leave from work.
The policy applies to permanent, temporary, contractual, and probationary employees unless an employment agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.
The Human Resources department is responsible for maintaining leave records and explaining leave procedures.
Employees should read this policy carefully and contact HR if they need clarification.
Leave is provided to support employee wellbeing, personal responsibilities, illness, and rest.
All leave requests must be submitted through the company-approved HR portal or other official process.
Employees should discuss planned leave with their reporting manager before making non-refundable travel arrangements.
Leave approval depends on eligibility, available balance, business requirements, and applicable law.
The company will administer leave fairly and consistently.
The leave year begins on the first day of January and ends on the last day of December.
Eligible employees receive casual leave according to their employment category and company policy.
Eligible employees receive earned leave according to their employment category and company policy.
Eligible employees receive sick leave according to their employment category and company policy.
The standard sample entitlement is twelve casual leave days per leave year.
The standard sample entitlement is twelve earned leave days per leave year.
The standard sample entitlement is eight sick leave days per leave year.
These sample entitlements may be adjusted to meet applicable statutory requirements and employment agreements.
Employees joining during the leave year may receive leave on a prorated basis.
The HR portal will display each employee's available leave balance.
Employees should check their leave balance before submitting a request.
Casual leave may be used for personal matters, family responsibilities, or other short-term needs.
Employees should submit casual leave requests at least three working days before the intended leave date whenever possible.
Casual leave may be requested for a full working day or a permitted partial day.
A manager may ask an employee to coordinate work coverage before approving planned casual leave.
Employees should not use casual leave as a substitute for reporting an absence without notice.
Unused casual leave will be handled according to the company's applicable carry-forward and expiry rules.
Casual leave cannot be treated as approved until confirmation is received.
Earned leave is intended for planned holidays, personal commitments, and extended rest.
Employees should submit earned leave requests at least seven calendar days before the intended start date whenever reasonably possible.
Longer periods of earned leave should be discussed with the reporting manager as early as possible.
Approval of earned leave may consider project schedules and available team coverage.
Employees should complete urgent assignments before starting earned leave.
Employees must provide a handover note when their absence may affect ongoing work.
Earned leave may be carried forward only within the limits permitted by company policy and applicable law.
Any leave encashment will be governed by the employee's employment terms and applicable law.
Employees should review their earned leave balance periodically.
Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work because of illness or injury.
Employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as possible when taking sick leave.
If the manager is unavailable, the employee should notify HR or the designated team contact.
Employees should provide an estimated return date when reasonably possible.
A medical certificate may be requested for extended or repeated periods of illness, subject to applicable law.
Medical information shared with HR will be handled confidentially.
Employees should not be required to disclose unnecessary medical details to colleagues.
Sick leave will be recorded according to the company's attendance and leave procedures.
Employees who become unwell during the workday should inform their manager before leaving, where practical.
The company may request appropriate supporting documents for extended sick leave, consistent with applicable law.
Employees who need additional medical leave should contact HR to discuss available options.
Employees should not work remotely when their health requires rest or medical attention.
Employees may request emergency leave for urgent and unforeseen personal or family circumstances.
Emergency leave should be reported to the reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.
Employees should provide the expected duration of emergency leave when they are able to do so.
The company may request reasonable supporting information where appropriate and lawful.
HR will help employees identify the appropriate leave category for an emergency.
Employees should update their manager if an emergency absence needs to be extended.
Maternity leave will be provided in accordance with applicable law and the employee's eligibility.
Employees planning maternity leave should contact HR as early as reasonably possible.
HR will explain the required documentation and available benefits in a confidential manner.
The company will not discriminate against an employee for taking legally protected maternity leave.
Employees returning from maternity leave should coordinate their return-to-work arrangements with HR and their manager.
Paternity or parental leave may be provided according to applicable law and the company's approved benefits.
Eligible employees should submit parental leave requests through the HR process.
HR will confirm the applicable entitlement and documentation requirements.
Bereavement leave may be considered when an employee experiences the death of an immediate family member.
Employees should inform their manager or HR when bereavement leave is needed.
The company will handle bereavement requests with sensitivity and respect.
The duration and eligibility for bereavement leave will follow company rules and applicable law.
Employees may request unpaid leave when paid leave balances are insufficient or another arrangement is necessary.
Unpaid leave requires approval from the appropriate manager and HR.
The effect of unpaid leave on salary, benefits, and service-related entitlements will be explained before approval where possible.
Unpaid leave will not be assumed to be approved merely because a request has been submitted.
Employees should discuss extended unpaid leave with HR before making commitments.
Employees who need leave for an official public duty, jury service, or other legally protected purpose should contact HR.
The company will provide statutory leave and related protections as required by applicable law.
Employees may be asked to provide reasonable documents for statutory leave where permitted by law.
Public holidays will be observed according to the company's published holiday calendar.
The HR department will publish the annual holiday calendar.
Employees assigned to work on a public holiday will be informed by their manager.
Compensatory time off, if applicable, will be administered under company policy and applicable law.
Employees should confirm holiday schedules with their team before planning leave.
Employees must submit leave requests using the official HR portal or designated form.
Each request should include the leave category, start date, end date, and a brief reason where required.
Employees should provide a contact method for urgent work matters when appropriate.
The system may send the request to the reporting manager for review.
The reporting manager will review work schedules and team coverage before making a decision.
HR may review requests to confirm eligibility and policy compliance.
Employees will receive an approval or rejection notification through the official process.
Employees should retain the approval notification for their records.
Employees must not make changes to attendance records to show leave that has not been approved.
If the HR portal is unavailable, employees should contact HR using an official communication channel.
A request submitted by email during a system outage must be entered into the HR portal when service is restored, if required.
Employees should cancel or amend a leave request promptly if their plans change.
A manager may ask an employee to adjust planned leave dates when essential business coverage is affected.
Any adjustment must be discussed with the employee and handled consistently with applicable law.
Managers should avoid unnecessary disruption to approved leave.
The company may contact an employee on leave only for genuinely urgent business matters, where appropriate.
Employees should not be expected to perform normal work duties while on approved leave.
Employees must arrange a reasonable handover before taking planned extended leave.
The handover should identify open tasks, deadlines, important contacts, and relevant document locations.
Employees must protect confidential information when preparing handover notes.
Employees should set an appropriate out-of-office message for extended leave.
The out-of-office message should identify an alternative contact for urgent matters where appropriate.
Managers should assign a temporary contact for essential responsibilities during an employee's absence.
Employees returning from leave should check with their manager for important updates.
Employees should report any leave balance or attendance discrepancy to HR promptly.
HR will investigate leave record discrepancies and correct confirmed errors.
Employees must not submit false leave requests or altered supporting documents.
Employees must not ask another person to submit a misleading leave request on their behalf.
Misuse of leave procedures may result in disciplinary action after a fair review.
Employees should notify HR if they believe a leave request has been handled inconsistently or unfairly.
Employees may raise a leave-related grievance through the official HR complaint process.
The company will handle leave-related medical and personal information confidentially.
Access to leave records will be limited to authorized personnel with a business need.
Managers should not disclose an employee's private reason for leave without authorization.
Employees must keep their contact details up to date so HR can communicate about leave administration.
Employees who transfer departments will retain leave balances according to company policy and applicable law.
Employees changing employment status should contact HR to understand any effect on leave eligibility.
Leave during a notice period must be requested and approved through the normal process.
Taking leave during a notice period does not automatically extend or shorten the notice period.
Any adjustment to the notice period must be confirmed in writing by HR.
On separation, unused leave will be settled according to the employment agreement, company policy, and applicable law.
Employees must return company property and complete required handover activities before their final working day.
Managers are responsible for reviewing leave requests promptly and consistently.
Managers should plan team workloads to allow employees to take eligible leave.
Managers must not discourage employees from using statutory or legally protected leave.
HR is responsible for maintaining accurate leave balances and policy records.
HR should inform employees when leave rules or statutory requirements change.
The company may update this policy from time to time, subject to applicable law and employment agreements.
Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the applicable law will prevail.
Employees should contact the Human Resources department for questions about leave eligibility, balances, approval, or documentation.
This document is a fictional sample created for educational and software development purposes.
ABC Limited is not a real company, and this document is not a legally reviewed employment policy.
